# Userspace NFS failure and recovery model

Design, 2026-09-09. Q8 means automatic recovery of every supported operation's enumerated crash windows when evidence and authority are available; it does not mean unlimited availability, recovery of destroyed bytes, or takeover based on silence. M1 covers storage replay, M2 overlay/session replay, M3 safe abandoned-session takeover and qualified Remote persistence. See [fencing](fencing-survey.md) and [syscall contract](syscall-matrix.md).

## Invariants, states and evidence

One session has one Umbra owner. Admission acquires the same backend authority before publishing a usable live session. A connection used for handshake/validation is not a second live session. Provider processes may recover under their still-live controller's authority only after old provider termination, tracee quiescence and outstanding-I/O disposition are established. A crashed controller leaves an abandoned session: M1/M2 do not autonomously steal its marker. Failed lease checks are sticky, not retried into mutation.

External edits are allowed and must become visible; they do not authorize another Umbra session. No external-edit prevention, automatic conflict repair or special protection of `control/`/`.provider` is promised. Malformed or externally changed recovery evidence is diagnosed, preserved and refused. Conflicting external writes during an interrupted operation are outside deterministic automatic reconciliation; absence of a conflict signal is not proof none occurred.

| State | Meaning and safe behavior |
| --- | --- |
| RUNNING | Authority, connection and required evidence are valid. |
| RECOVERING | Stop new mutations and quiesce/stop the tree; resolve bounded outstanding operations. No success, release or clean checkpoint is fabricated. |
| BLOCKED_RECOVERABLE | Timeout, missing authority or unavailable evidence; retain writer marker, replay data and diagnostic. Resume only after recovery/authority conditions change; not marked corrupted solely for outage. |
| CORRUPTED | Contradictory identities, invalid complete frames/interior checksums, or demonstrated loss of previously promised durable data. Preserve remaining bytes; refuse automatic repair/relaunch. |
| FAILED_TRACEE | Agent/child failure recorded; storage may remain intact. No clean checkpoint inferred from exit. |

“Safely” means no host-write fallback, no two active owners, no reapplication of a recorded committed mutation, no guessed overwrite/rollback, and no release that permits uncertain old I/O to overlap a new owner. Already durable data must survive within the qualified deployment's failure model. In-flight operations may have partial effects and cannot be silently reported as having had none. Bytes never received or durably recorded cannot be reconstructed; unflushed app buffers/private COW pages are not a storage promise.

Required persistent evidence (schema must preserve readable existing runs; no migration namespace): run/base/format identities; writer ID/epoch and admission/release phase; operation ID/key and exact request; object/parent identities and preconditions; durable intent and immutable bounded write payload or reference; completed result including short count/error; original error latch; overlay prepare/observed/commit sequence; checkpoint digest/publication state. Retry results survive restarts. Legacy completed records remain readable; legacy ambiguous intents with insufficient evidence stop as BLOCKED_RECOVERABLE rather than being guessed or silently migrated.

Volatile evidence: NFS client/open/lock state, RPC XID, expected reply, write verifier and pending buffers. These are not authority after process restart. Persist enough payload/identity before mutation to rebuild outcomes independently of a lost RPC reply cache. Persist hashes alone only when a surviving payload source is guaranteed. New implementation keeps a bounded replay queue; capacity exhaustion applies backpressure before dispatch. A same-host scratch log may aid diagnosis but cannot be the sole cross-machine recovery source.

## Crash/outage taxonomy

Every row retains the common evidence above. “Stop” means BLOCKED_RECOVERABLE unless corruption is positively established; when storage is unreachable retain a local diagnostic and any unsent payload without pretending a remote state marker was written.

| Fault | Recovery action | Additional evidence / safe stopping point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Umbra supervisor crash | Independent enforcement stops/terminates descendants. Recover journal and primitives without assuming owner release. M3 fences abandoned owner before relaunch; M1/M2 await authorized cooperative/operator recovery. | Supervisor incarnation, last committed sequence, controller-loss evidence. Stop if old tree/provider/RPCs cannot be ruled out. |
| Storage provider crash | Quiesce tracees; controller retains ownership. Establish old provider dead and uncertain RPC disposition; reconnect, rebuild NFS state and reconcile intents. | Controller/provider incarnation and pending calls. No second independently acquired writer lease; otherwise use abandoned-owner rule. |
| Tracee crash / SIGKILL | Quiesce remaining descendants, reconcile observed/unknown syscall results, flush surviving shared dirty state if supported. Record failed execution; do not treat agent buffers as persisted. | Exit/signal and syscall-entry/result boundary; M2 mapped-dirty evidence. Storage corruption is not inferred from a nonzero exit. |
| Transient network partition | Stop mutation admission; bounded reconnect and protocol replay, then validate authority, identities and verifiers before continuing. | Original RPC/request identities, partial counts, payloads. Never resubmit a non-idempotent namespace operation under a new identity merely because its reply was lost. |
| Prolonged network partition | After outage budget, leave run blocked and tree stopped/terminated; hold marker and expose recovery-required status. | Elapsed monotonic budget and original error. No automatic marker deletion or successful close on timeout. Recovery can be retried later; waiting is bounded, data retention is not silently expired. |
| Ganesha graceful restart | Reconnect; treat client/open state as needing validation/reclaim; honor grace. Verify all unstable writes rather than assuming graceful shutdown preserved them. | Server boot/recovery identity, grace responses, verifier changes, reclaim results. Stop if reclaim cannot establish valid object state. |
| Ganesha hard restart | Same, plus rebuild from durable intents/payloads; compare WRITE/COMMIT verifiers and replay only after correct authority/order. | Recovery backend continuity, server logs and wire trace in qualification. Process restart alone is not power-loss qualification. |
| Server ENOSPC / quota | Report actual error/short count, retain intent and partial effects; block uncertain mutations. Retry recovery when capacity returns, without replaying already committed results. | Space/quota response and affected range/record. A recorded completed error stays the result for that key; new work uses a new operation identity. |
| Server EIO / failed stable write | Latch original write/barrier failure. Do not issue a clean receipt or release. Inspect recoverable ranges after service restoration. | Failed range, requested/returned stability and server status. Mark corrupted only on proven contradiction/loss; otherwise blocked. A later successful flush cannot erase lost-write evidence. |
| Stale filehandle / FHEXPIRED | Re-resolve only if persistent object identity proves the same object. Never reopen “the same path” onto a replacement for an existing handle. | Old fsid/fileid/handle generation and parent/name evidence. If identity cannot be proven, stop; external replacement is normal for new opens, not permission to retarget old handles. |
| SIGKILL of client/controller | Same as corresponding process crash, with no destructor/close assumptions. | Durable evidence only; ownership marker retained. |
| SIGSTOP then SIGCONT | No heartbeat assumption while stopped. On resume, recheck local deadline and remote state before any queued mutation; expired authority poisons session. M3 also rejects it server-side after takeover. | Monotonic suspend gap, epoch, pending RPCs. If queued remote effects cannot be excluded, stay blocked. |
| Client power loss during WRITE/COMMIT | Rebuild using server-retained intent/payload; volatile acknowledged-but-unstable data is not enough. M3 authority gate precedes mutation replay. | Last durable payload and verifier association. If payload was not durable, do not claim recovery of those bytes. |
| Server/storage power loss during WRITE/COMMIT | M3 verifies persistence assumptions of server/export/filesystem/device; recover changed-verifier writes from durable payload if available. | Independent expected digests, before/after wire traces and cold-client/server-side observations. Loss after a qualified durable receipt is CORRUPTED and a qualification failure. |
| Split brain: two hosts believe they own | Server-atomic admission denies contender. On conflicting valid ownership evidence, both stop; M3 selected fencing authority excludes old mutations before selecting a winner. | Competing owner IDs/epochs, fence receipt and cutoff. Client-side lock-file checks and increasing epochs alone are insufficient. |

NFS WRITE returns stability/verifier information; COMMIT verifier handling is separate from namespace journaling. [RFC 7530 WRITE](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7530.html#section-16.32), [COMMIT](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7530.html#section-16.3) and [client retransmission](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7530.html#section-3.1.1) are protocol references; the stronger staged guarantees above are Umbra design requirements, not guarantees inferred from an fsync-shaped API.

## Finite budgets and replay acceptance

Initial design defaults: RPC attempt deadline 5 seconds, reconnect backoff 250 ms doubling to 5 seconds, ordinary outage budget 30 seconds. Grace recovery has a separate bounded budget of configured grace + 30 seconds (120 seconds for a 90-second grace). No mutation resumes merely to keep a lease alive. Authority renewal runs independently of blocking trace reads; use a margin no longer than one third of the shorter local/NFS lease. IPC deadlines must allow the selected operation budget plus a small framing margin, or return an explicit pending/blocked result without pretending cancellation. Record effective settings in qualification; tune performance only without weakening safety.

For each supported primitive and overlay transaction inject interruption: before intent, after durable intent, after partial server effect, after effect/before result, after durable result/before reply, and after overlay commit/before tracee resume. Reopen and compare exact bytes, identity, namespace and replay results. Include exclusive create/verifier replay; short writes; rename replacement/whiteouts; unlink/open-object lifetime; marker/epoch acquisition and release; checkpoint publication. Already completed keys return recorded outcomes. Incomplete operations require proof of before/after state or a recoverable staging protocol; multi-op COMPOUND is not a transaction. Do not substitute “requires reconciliation” for implemented recovery in a supported new-format crash window.

M1 can exercise these with provider/fake-controller callers and two userspace clients on an isolated Ganesha export. M2 adds real tracees and mappings. M3 separately proves lost-reply, stopped-old-client, server restart and actual storage power-loss/fencing cases. None may access `~/umbra-scratch/nfs/mnt/umbra-nfs`, invoke a mounted NFS suite or discover existing scratch infrastructure. This document schedules future work only.
